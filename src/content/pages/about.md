I'm Brandon Morris, a senior software engineer currently located in Silicon Valley. My areas of experience and interest lie in **automated mobile testing** (iOS and Android), **machine learning**, and **software infrastructure**.

## Background

In 2018 I received my undergraduate degrees in Software Engineering and Applied Mathematics from Auburn University, where I also conducted research under [Dr. Anh Nguyen](http://anhnguyen.me/) studying deep learning robustness against [adversarial attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.08529). Before that, I researched [High Performance Computing methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.04788) under Dr. Anthony Skjellum and [GPU-accelerated chemistry simulations](https://github.com/orlandoacevedo/MCGPU) under Dr. Jeffrey Overbey and Dr. Orlando Acevedo.

For a full year I worked as a software engineering co-op at ADTRAN, Inc., rotating through teams in quality assurance, internal IaaS testing infrastructure, and cloud networking cluster management technology. After graduation, I interned at Sandia National Labs where I engineered a machine learning model to predict hard drive failure from raw S.M.A.R.T. disk attributes.

## This Blog

I write about machine learning, software engineering, and things I find interesting — tutorials, paper summaries, and technical deep-dives.
